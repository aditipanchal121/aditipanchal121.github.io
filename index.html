About Me

Hi! My name is Aditi. I am an Applied Scientist / Machine Learning Engineer with ~4 years of experience. My full-time experience has been at Microsoft, where I worked on Copilot evaluation and experimentation (creating and productionizing evaluation models, end-to-end for Enterprise Copilot). I recently left my role at Microsoft to pursue my M.S. in Computer Science, ML Specialization at Georgia Tech, in an effort to focus on research, as well as to solidify my ML theory knowledge and expand my breadth within the field. I also have a B.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University, with minors in Statistics and Psychology. I graduated with Engineering Honors and did ML research, some of which was published. My experience spans software engineering, machine learning, and data science, and I am passionate about building products that leverage AI for social good. Some of my projects are linked below, but you can also visit my GitHub to see all of them.

Outside of school and work, I love to get involved in the community through volunteering. I also work on ML projects that explore interesting applications of AI: check out my personal project that uses ML to predict low/high blood sugar events for diabetics, using time-series models (forecasting and fine-tuned foundation models). I also travel, read, rock climb, dance, take pictures, and watch movies (I am watching several of the "Classics" these days for the first time).

I would love to hear from you about AS / MLE opportunities, or if you would simply like to get in touch! I am currently looking for Summer 2027 internships. See my resume here!
